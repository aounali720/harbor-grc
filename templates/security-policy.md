# Information security policy — adaptable draft

## Purpose and scope

Protect [business services/data] through defined responsibilities, proportionate safeguards, and reviewed risk decisions. Applies to [people, systems, locations, suppliers]. Exclusions and interfaces: [scope and reasons]. Related obligations: [validated contract/regulatory/organizational requirements and their owners].

## Requirements

1. Maintain an owned inventory of in-scope assets, suppliers, data flows, and classifications. Reconcile it against [authoritative sources] at [frequency] and after material change.
2. Assess material security scenarios using [approved method]. Assign an accountable business owner, treatment, due date, and review triggers. Acceptance needs independent authorized approval, rationale, compensating measures, and expiry.
3. Grant access through approved identities and least privilege. Define MFA, privileged access, joiner/mover/leaver, and periodic review requirements in the access standard. Verify revocation.
4. Protect data in storage and transit according to its classification. Keep credentials in approved secret systems. Define retention, deletion, and authorized sharing for each relevant data class.
5. Review and test production changes. Maintain approved cloud configuration baselines, logging, vulnerability response, and recovery procedures. Record justified exceptions with expiry.
6. Complete supplier assessment before new data access or integration. Reassess on material changes and according to approved tier intervals. Verify offboarding.
7. Report suspected incidents through [approved route]. Preserve evidence and follow the incident plan. Relevant legal/privacy/communications owners assess notification obligations.
8. Retain control evidence in [approved repository], protect it against unauthorized change, and obtain qualified independent review. Track failures as findings with measurable closure conditions.
9. Test service recovery and GRC application restoration at [frequency] against approved objectives. Record measured results and gaps.

## Accountability and exceptions

[Executive sponsor] approves scope, appetite, and resources. [GRC lead] coordinates reporting. Service/control owners operate and evidence controls. Reviewers challenge results and preserve limitations. Application administrators manage technical access and recovery; their role does not confer business acceptance authority.

Exceptions use [exception process/template]. The requester cannot approve their own exception. Breaches of policy follow [appropriate organizational process]. Do not invent punitive terms without HR/legal review.
