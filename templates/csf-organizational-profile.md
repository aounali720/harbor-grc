## Fictional worked example — Northstar supplier prioritization

**Selected reference:** GV.SC-04, project topic “supplier criticality.” This identifier is a reference; the following target is Northstar's proposed implementation.

**Scope:** production integrations. **Current:** the service owner has five suppliers on a draft list; identity/integration records have not been reconciled and criticality is partly unreviewed. **Evidence:** a synthetic intake note, not a real supplier assessment. **Gap:** completeness and prioritization cannot yet be demonstrated.

**Target:** reconcile the supplier population against approved procurement and integration sources; have the accountable owner review data/access/criticality for every in-scope supplier; record tier and dated approval of the inventory. **Action:** GRC lead coordinates reconciliation; service owners decide criticality; reviewer checks discrepancies. **Due:** [set a real date]. **Completion evidence:** reconciliation, decisions, and residual unknowns.

Do not report this example as achieved, infer a Tier from it, or claim full GV.SC coverage. Repeat the method for the other selected outcomes.
